☀️ Megha Solar Solutions

Solar Service & PM Surya Ghar Assistance Platform

Megha Solar Solutions is a full-stack web platform designed to simplify the journey from understanding solar energy to applying for, coordinating, and completing solar installation.

The platform focuses on solar installation and service coordination in Meghalaya, while helping customers understand the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, evaluate their electricity usage, explore potential savings, and request relevant solar services.

«From understanding solar to getting it installed — Megha Solar Solutions helps make the process simpler.»

---

🚀 Project Overview

The traditional solar-installation process can involve multiple steps — understanding the scheme, checking electricity usage, determining suitability, requesting installation, coordinating services, and maintaining the system afterward.

Megha Solar Solutions brings these activities into a single digital platform.

The system is designed around:

- ☀️ Solar installation assistance
- 🏠 PM Surya Ghar scheme guidance
- 📊 Electricity-usage analysis
- 💰 Solar savings and payback estimation
- 🔧 Solar maintenance and repair services
- ♨️ Solar water heater installation & services
- 💡 Solar/electrical project services
- 🏛️ Government tender-related services
- 📋 Customer application management
- 🗺️ Service availability across Meghalaya
- 👨‍💼 Customer and administrator dashboards

📊 Personalized Electricity Analysis

One of the major features of the platform is personalized electricity-usage analysis.

Customers can provide historical electricity information, allowing the system to analyze approximately 2–5 years of available usage/billing history, where sufficient data exists.

The analysis can present:

- Electricity consumption trends
- Electricity expenditure
- Estimated solar generation
- Potential electricity offset
- Estimated savings
- Approximate payback period
- Visual charts and graphs
- Data-driven explanation of potential solar value

This turns raw electricity-bill information into something easier for customers to understand.

---

🧮 Solar Calculator

The platform includes a solar calculation concept that allows users to compare estimated economics before and after adopting solar.

The calculator can be used to explore:

- Estimated system cost
- Scheme-related benefits
- Estimated electricity savings
- Long-term financial impact
- Approximate payback period

All estimates are presented as indicative calculations, not guaranteed financial outcomes.
